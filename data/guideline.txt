4.2 Dyspepsia and NSAID-associated gastritis. Patients presenting with epigastric burning pain who are taking a non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID) should have the NSAID stopped where possible. A proton pump inhibitor (PPI) at standard dose for four to eight weeks is recommended. Test for Helicobacter pylori using a stool antigen test; the patient should not have taken a PPI in the preceding two weeks as this reduces test sensitivity. Alarm features requiring urgent referral: haematemesis, melaena, unintentional weight loss, persistent vomiting, dysphagia, age over 55 with new-onset symptoms. Patients who require ongoing NSAID therapy should receive PPI co-prescription for gastroprotection.
